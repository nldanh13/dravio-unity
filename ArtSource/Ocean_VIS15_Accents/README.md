# VIS.15 — Ocean Bright Fantasy accents

Three original 3D source candidates: thick curved seagrass ribbons for Estuary, hollow tube sponges in coral/apricot for Reef, cool pearl anemone with rounded tentacles for Cool Pool. No new textures or gameplay. Existing Ocean art and its 15 spawns, 6 rest points, 3 swim volumes, 3 walk areas and 13 interactions untouched. No replacements of existing collidable coral/table/rock/anemone or rest surfaces. Only optional dressing after terrain, visibility, and full swim-volume checks. Sea grass static for now; no assumption of shader wind. Pearl is purely decorative, not reward or collectible; no emission map. 

Reproduce `node scripts/art/environment/build_ocean_vis15_accents.mjs <new-dir>`. One of each adds 1492 triangles to manifest baseline 19660, conditional only. Each has one source vertex-color material; proposed shared white-base opaque material (if approved), preserve RGB, set alpha 0 and suitable UV during FBX conversion, not the existing tinted atlas. Existing Ocean 60000 tris, 10 materials, 6 MiB RGBA32+mips limit unchanged. No additional texture budget requested. Internal geometry and deterministic checks only; actual visual quality, occlusion, watertight manifold, Blender, glTF validator, Unity and Android NOT RUN. Source not production approved.
