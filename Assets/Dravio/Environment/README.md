# U1-ENV — Origin Sanctuary environment kit

Original, fully procedural environment art for Dravio's starting area (Khởi Nguyên / Origin Sanctuary): Ancient Fantasy, cozy, stylized, painterly. Every mesh and texture is generated by the scripts in `scripts/art/environment/`. No photo, scan, store asset or third-party texture is used, so the kit can live in this public repository.

This kit is separate from gameplay. It does not touch Emberling, its Animator, the C# domain, or the existing test scenes (`OriginMeadow`, `AncientSanctuary`, `EmberlingModelPreview`, `EmberlingLivingPreview`).

## Contents

| Folder | What |
| --- | --- |
| `Models/` | 25 FBX files (`SM_ENV_*`), 1 m = 1 unit, pivot at base centre, front faces the camera (-Z) |
| `Textures/` | Painterly atlas 1024², emission 512², foliage cards 512² (alpha), ground 512² (tiling), water 256² (tiling) |
| `Shaders/` | `Dravio/Environment/Painterly Lit` and `Painterly Water` (URP 17, mobile) |
| `Scripts/SanctuaryCameraFit.cs` | Landscape framing of the fixed orthographic camera, with the real Fantasy UI width and the Safe Area (visual only) |
| `Scripts/Living/` | U1-ENV.3 living environment: time of day, wind, water ripples, particles, ambience, interaction points |
| `Audio/` | U1-ENV.3 ambience: 6 seamless loops and 6 one-shots, synthesised by `scripts/art/environment/ambience_audio.py` |
| `Editor/` | Import rules (this folder only) and the **Dravio > Environment** menu |
| `Data/` | `OriginSanctuaryLayout.json` (scene layout), `OriginSanctuaryLocations.json`, `OriginSanctuaryColliders.json`, `OriginSanctuaryLiving.json` (U1-ENV.3), `OriginSanctuaryManifest.json` (triangles, materials, budgets) |
| `Materials/`, `Prefabs/`, `Scenes/` | Generated in Unity by the menu (commit them after checking) |

### Modular assets

- Columns and ruined walls: `Column_Intact`, `Column_Broken`, `Column_Fallen`, `Wall_Ruin_A`, `Wall_Ruin_B`
- Ancient dragon statue: `Statue_Dragon` (seated guardian with folded wings, horns and softly glowing eyes, on a gold-trimmed plinth)
- Old trees, roots and vines: `Tree_Ancient`, `Root_Cluster`, `Vine_Hanging` (pivot at the top, hang it from a ledge)
- Mossy rocks: `Rock_Mossy_A/B/C`
- Grass, flowers, mushrooms: `Grass_Tuft_A/B`, `Flower_Patch_Pink/Blue/Gold`, `Fern`, `Mushroom_Cluster_Red`, `Mushroom_Cluster_Glow`

- Ground sheet: `Ground_Sanctuary` (painted earth path and soft shade under the big props, in vertex colours)

## Mobile budget

- About 24,000 triangles for the whole layout (164 placements), against a 60,000 budget. Biggest assets: statue 2.7k, tree 2.2k, ground 2.2k, gate 1.2k.
- 4 materials for the entire environment: `M_ENV_Props` (every opaque prop through one atlas), `M_ENV_Foliage` (alpha-clip cards), `M_ENV_Ground`, `M_ENV_Water`.
- Every prefab is Batching Static. With static batching and the SRP Batcher, same-material renderers merge, so draw calls stay close to the number of materials times shadow/depth passes rather than the number of objects.
- Small items (grass, flowers, ferns, mushrooms, small rocks, pond, vines, ground) do not cast shadows. Light and reflection probes are off on the kit.
- Textures: 5 maps, about 1.9 million texels in total. That is roughly 1.1 MB on the phone with ASTC 6×6 (Android and iOS) and mipmaps, instead of about 10 MB uncompressed. Alpha coverage is preserved in the foliage mipmaps.
- Shaders: main light, SH ambient and fog only. No normal maps, no depth or opaque texture. Emission pulse and grass wind are computed in the shader, so no per-frame scripts run.
- The Blender build fails if an asset goes over its triangle budget or any atlas UV leaves its tile.

## Painterly look

- The atlas tiles are painted with seeded brush-stroke stamps over posterized value noise, with hand-placed-looking cracks, mortar, moss dabs, bark grooves and invented rune sigils.
- Vertex colour RGB holds painted ambient occlusion (darker at the base of every prop). Alpha holds the wind weight for foliage, or the earth-path mask for the ground.
- `Painterly Lit` uses a soft two-tone light ramp, a cool shadow tint, a warm rim, and pulsing emission for the runes, the keystone, the statue's eyes and the glowing mushrooms.

## Regenerate the art (Blender 5.3)

From the repository root (PowerShell):

```powershell
& "C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.3\blender.exe" --factory-startup --background --python-exit-code 1 `
  --python scripts/art/environment/build_origin_sanctuary.py -- `
  --out art-exports/environment --unity Assets/Dravio/Environment --preview
```

- `--out` is the working folder (ignored by git): FBX, `OriginSanctuaryKit.blend` (all assets side by side, editable), textures, manifest and preview render.
- `--unity` copies only into `Assets/Dravio/Environment/{Models,Textures,Data}`.
- `--preview` renders `OriginSanctuary_preview_16x9.png` and `_20x9.png` from the layout camera with Cycles on the CPU. It takes about 30 seconds and can be left out.
- Python checks without Blender (needs numpy): `python -m unittest scripts/art/environment/test_environment_kit.py`

The layout lives in `scripts/art/environment/layout.py`, in Unity coordinates. Change it there, re-run the build, then rebuild the scene.

## Build the scene in Unity

1. Close Play Mode. Let Unity import `Assets/Dravio/Environment` and check that the Console has no red errors. The import rules in `Editor/EnvironmentImportSettings.cs` set scale, compression and ASTC automatically.
2. Choose **Dravio > Environment > Build Everything (Materials, Prefabs, Scene)**, or run steps 1, 2 and 3 separately:
   1. *Setup Materials and Import Remaps* creates the 4 materials and remaps the FBX material names (`ENV_Props` and the others) to them.
   2. *Build Prefabs* writes `Prefabs/SM_ENV_*.prefab` with static flags and shadow settings.
   3. *Create or Open Origin Sanctuary Scene* creates `Scenes/OriginSanctuary_Environment.unity` from the layout JSON. If the scene already exists, it opens it and keeps your edits. *Rebuild ... (overwrite)* regenerates it after asking.
3. Look at the Game view at 16:9, 19.5:9 and 20:9 (landscape only), and press Play to see the wind and the glow pulse.
4. Run **Dravio > Environment > Report Mobile Budget** and check the Frame Debugger for batches.
5. Commit the generated `Materials/`, `Prefabs/`, `Scenes/` and every `.meta` file.

If the Painterly shader fails to compile on your machine, the tool falls back to URP/Lit materials, says so in the Console, and the scene still builds. Report the shader error so it can be fixed.

## Landscape and habitats (U1-ENV.2)

The sanctuary is landscape only. It is framed for 16:9, 19.5:9 and 20:9 phones, with the right part of the screen kept free for the Fantasy UI panel.

- **Camera.** `SanctuaryCameraFit` keeps the play area to the left of a panel that is `rightPanelScreenHeights` (0.5) screen heights wide, plus a 0.05 left margin. The camera shifts right by the same world distance at every aspect, so wider phones only show more dressing on the left and under the panel. Below 16:9 it zooms out instead of cropping. In portrait it logs one warning.
- **Habitats.** Three microhabitats tile the play area without gaps, with ids and unlock rules matching the domain data:
  - `origin_meadow` (centre, open by default): flowers, clover, tall grass, the runestones and the gate clearing.
  - `origin_pondbank` (left, needs `DISC_AQUATIC_HABITAT`): the pond, bank ring, stepping stones, reeds and cattails. Swimmers may enter the swim area.
  - `origin_warm_rocks` (right of the meadow, needs `DISC_EMBERLING_THERMAL`): warm flat and stacked rocks, dry grass and twigs.
- **Location data.** `Data/OriginSanctuaryLocations.json` is the source of truth. **Dravio > Environment > 4. Update Location Data** turns it into `Data/OriginSanctuaryLocations.asset` (`SanctuaryLocationData`). It holds habitat polygons, the walkable rectangle, obstacle footprints, 13 spawn points, 4 transitions between habitats and perch points. Living World can call `HabitatAt`, `IsWalkable(pos, habitatId, canSwim)`, `SpawnPointsIn`, `TransitionsFrom` and `PerchesIn`. The scene also gets `SanctuaryMarker` objects and a gizmo drawer so designers can see the points.
- **Colliders.** `Data/OriginSanctuaryColliders.json` lists box colliders per asset. Prefabs get them during *Build Prefabs*; the ground gets one flat box. Small props have none.
- **Validation.** **Dravio > Environment > 5. Validate Landscape Framing and UI Area** checks, with a real camera at the three aspects, that every habitat corner, spawn, transition and perch is inside the free area, that every spawn is walkable, and reports (but never changes) the Player orientation.
- **Variants and performance.** New assets: a second tree, a round shrub, warm rocks (flat and stacked), a pond bank ring, stepping stones and 8 new foliage cards (reed, cattail, dry grass, tall meadow grass, clover, lilac flowers, shrub leaves, dry twigs). Ground cover is baked into one mesh per habitat plus one for the edges, so the scene has 45 placements instead of 164 (about 26k triangles). Everything is static and uses the same 4 materials.

To check framing without Unity, `scripts/art/environment/preview_overlay.py` draws the habitats, points and UI panel over the Blender previews.

The project's Player orientation is not changed by these tools. Set **Player > Resolution and Presentation > Default Orientation** to Landscape Left yourself (it is a project setting outside Environment).

## Living environment (U1-ENV.3)

Everything here is cosmetic. There is no weather, disaster or any other system that changes gameplay, and nothing is saved. The U1-ENV.2 layout, colliders, location data and IDs are unchanged (the tests check their exact file hashes).

**Data.** `Data/OriginSanctuaryLiving.json` (from `scripts/art/environment/living.py`) becomes `Data/OriginSanctuaryLiving.asset` (`SanctuaryLivingData`) through **Dravio > Environment > 6. Update Living Environment Data**. It holds the day phases, 14 time-of-day keyframes, 15 interaction points, particle volumes and ambience emitters.

**Scene.** **7. Add or Update Living Environment in Scene** adds one root, *Origin Sanctuary - Living Environment*, to `Scenes/OriginSanctuary_Environment.unity` only. Re-running it replaces just that root. *Build Everything* now includes steps 6 and 7. To preview, scrub `hour` on **Environment Time Of Day** in edit mode.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `EnvironmentTimeOfDay` | Dawn 5:00 to 7:00, day to 17:30, dusk to 19:30, night until 5:00. It drives the one directional light (sun by day, moon by night), the trilight ambient (written straight into the ambient probe), fog and the camera background, and sets `_DravioLiving` (night glow on runes and glow caps, a lower ambient floor at night). The clock can be **Simulated** (24 real minutes per day by default), **DeviceClock** or **External** (the game calls `SetHour`). 13:00 is exactly the U1-ENV.2 lighting. |
| `EnvironmentWind` | A calm base that follows the time of day, plus soft Perlin gusts. It sets `_DravioWind`, so foliage sways harder and leans downwind and the pond pattern drifts. Gusts also drive the dust motes and the wind loop. |
| `EnvironmentWater` | Up to 4 expanding ripple rings computed in the water shader (`AddRipple`, `AddSplash`). There are no extra meshes or render textures. |
| `EnvironmentAmbientParticles` | Fireflies (meadow and pond, dusk and night), light dust motes (day and golden hour) and small pond rings, plus two burst systems for interaction feedback. High tier caps: 36 + 14 fireflies, 48 dust motes, 8 rings. |
| `EnvironmentAudio` | Six loops (day birds, night crickets, wind, pond water, night frogs, warm-rock insects) mixed by the time of day. The pond and warm-rock loops fade with distance from a focus point (`SetFocus`, for example the active dragon) and pan with screen position. One-shots use a 2 to 4 voice pool. Route `output` to the game's ambience mixer group. |
| `EnvironmentInteractions` | Gives out interaction points (`FindNearest`, `TryReserve`, `Release`, `ReleaseAll`) and plays the cosmetic response on `NotifyUsed`: a ripple, splash, petals, rune sparkles, heat motes, a dust puff or fireflies, plus a sound. Occupants are plain ints, so it does not depend on the character code. |
| `LivingEnvironment` | One entry point (`LivingEnvironment.Active`) that holds all of the parts above. |
| `EnvironmentQuality` | Low, Medium or High, detected from memory and core count, and settable by the game. It scales particle counts (0.35 / 0.65 / 1), update rate (4 to 10 Hz), one-shot voices, and turns moon shadows off on Low. |

**Interaction points** (each with an approach position, facing, target, preferred phases, animation hint, feedback and sound):

- Pondbank: 2 drink, 1 splash (swimmers only, target in the swim area), 1 watch-the-glow-mushrooms.
- Meadow: 2 inspect rune, 2 sniff flowers, 1 stargaze (night), 1 rest.
- Warm rocks: 3 bask, 1 dig, 1 inspect statue.

Every approach point is walkable ground in its habitat, at least 0.8 m from the spawn points, outside the transition triggers, and inside the free screen area for all six device cases in the tests.

**Camera API for the Fantasy UI and the Safe Area.** `SanctuaryCameraFit` now takes real numbers:

- `SetFantasyUiOccluderPixels(px)` takes how many pixels the panel covers from the right edge. `SetFantasyUiPanel(rectTransform, uiCamera)` tracks a panel every frame. The easiest route is to add **`SanctuaryUiInsetSource`** to the panel; this needs no UI code.
- It reads `Screen.safeArea` (notch, rounded corners, home bar). `SetSafeAreaOverride` exists for tests.
- `FreeViewport` and `FreeViewportChanged` tell other systems which part of the screen is free. `ComputeFraming` is the pure function behind it, mirrored in `scripts/art/environment/framing.py`.

The size only ever grows (it never zooms in), just enough to keep the 16:9 design width and height free, and the free area is re-centred. With no report and no insets the pose is exactly the U1-ENV.2 one.

**Audio licence.** Every clip is synthesised from noise and oscillators by `scripts/art/environment/ambience_audio.py`. No recordings, samples or third-party sounds are used. Clips are 22.05 kHz mono WAV; on import, loops become Vorbis compressed in memory and one-shots become ADPCM.

**Checks.**

- **8. Validate Living Environment** checks interaction ids, habitats and walkability, and that splash targets are in the swim area. It also checks framing for the design, notch-left, notch-both-plus-home-bar and wide-UI cases at the three aspects, that all audio clips exist, and that 13:00 matches U1-ENV.2.
- Python: `python -m unittest scripts/art/environment/test_living_environment.py` (numpy and Pillow).

Previews: `scripts/art/environment/preview/OriginSanctuary_preview_dusk_16x9.png` and `_night_16x9.png` are Blender renders lit from the same keyframes, with fireflies.

## Known limits

- These are the first production-direction assets, generated rather than hand-sculpted. An artist pass (hand-painted details, extra variants, LODs if the camera ever zooms) is still welcome.
- Colliders are simple boxes for the large props only. There is no NavMesh; walkability is answered by `SanctuaryLocationData.IsWalkable`.
- The Painterly shader has no Meta pass, so it is meant for realtime lighting. Lightmap baking is not supported yet.
- The scene is not added to Build Settings automatically.
- U1-ENV.3 runtime code, shader changes, particles and audio import have not been compiled or run in Unity from this environment. See the PR for the list of local checks.
- The painterly shader only uses the main light, so there are no extra point lights at night. Glowing props brighten through the night glow instead.
- The ambient probe is built from the trilight colours in code. The exact brightness should be checked by eye in Unity, and `ambientScale` exists for tuning.
- The ambience is procedural. It sets the mood, but a sound designer may want to replace or layer it later. The ids and the mixer routing stay the same.
